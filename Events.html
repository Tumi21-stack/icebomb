Events & Functions

For an affordable sweet treat for your festivities, why not hire our mobile vending unit for your next big private or corporate event? The delicious range of Ice Bomb fruit lollies have been given out at launches, year-end events, weddings, birthday parties, and brand activations!

We can custom-wrap a glass-top freezer with your branding on it for your event. Alternatively, you can hire our mobile vending freezer trailer. Our well-dressed and dedicated staff member will be on-site to hand out the Ice Bombs to your guests.

For more information, email info@icebombs.co.za or call +27 772 307 067.
