How to Become a Stockist

We supply stockists with our delicious range of real fruit lollies throughout South Africa. Our lollies are all handmade in small batches using real fruit. They are available in the following flavours: granadilla, mango, litchi, lemon, strawberry and pineapple.

The Ice Bombs weigh 150g each, they are vegan-friendly and Halaal- and Kosher-certified. They come fully packaged in colourful packaging, and are sold in bags of 50 units per flavour.

The minimum order to qualify for wholesale prices is 150 units. However, if you would like us to supply you with a sign-written glass-top display freezer in which to hold the stock, then the minimum opening order is 300 units.
