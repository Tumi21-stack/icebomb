About Us

The Ice Bomb range of real fruit lollies has seen tremendous growth since it was launched in June 2016, by real fruit lolly pioneers, Antonella & Miklos Dei. The idea for the Ice Bomb range came from Antonella making lollies as a way of getting her kids to eat more fruit.

Soon, the duo were getting requests for the lollies from all over, so they decided to knuckle down and launch the range commercially, and as they say, the rest is history!

Since its launch, the Ice Bomb range has grown from strength to strength. The range is currently distributed to stockists nationwide, as well as to Namibia and Mozambique.

Going forward, Antonella and Miklos aim to expand Ice Bomb's national footprint, and hopefully start exporting the range to other international markets.
